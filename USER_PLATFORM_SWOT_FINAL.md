## 2. Competitive Moats

1. **High-Fidelity Document Parity**: True layout parity between browser preview, binary PDF, and Microsoft Word DOCX across 51 unique templates.
2. **Contextual AI Intelligence Engine**: AI summary and work description generation dynamically utilizes candidate's years of experience, skills, certifications, and target job titles without generic hallucination.
3. **Enterprise-Grade Security Architecture**: Absolute owner-scoped MariaDB queries, zero plain-text secrets in browser memory, and complete cryptographic audit logging.
